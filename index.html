Ace Attorney Fun Facts: Development & Origins

  • Nearly Scrapped: Capcom producers almost canceled the first game before creator Shu Takumi fought to finish it.
  • Game Boy Roots: The series originally started as a standalone concept for the Game Boy Color before moving to the Game Boy Advance.
  • Last-Minute Addition: The very first case's culprit and Larry Butz were added late in development due to strict cartridge memory limits.
  • Personal Experience:Phoenix Wright’s childhood flashback—where he is falsely accused of stealing lunch money and defended by young Edgeworth and Larry—is based on Shu Takumi's own elementary school memories.
  • A Demon Judge:The opening of Justice for All was originally supposed to feature a demon instead of the Judge, inspired by Takumi playing Devil May Cry.
  • Capcom Voice Cast:The iconic "Objection!", "Take That!", and "Hold It!" voice clips in the original DS releases were recorded by Capcom staff members, including Hideki Kamiya as Godot.

Top 5: Characters from Ace Attorney

  1. Phoenix Wright: The main protagonist of the series, a defense attorney known for his sharp mind and determination to seek justice.
  2. Mia Fey: Phoenix's mentor and a skilled attorney who plays a crucial role in his development as a lawyer.
  3. Apollo Justice: A young defense attorney who brings a fresh perspective to the courtroom and has his own unique abilities.
  4. Athena Cykes: A brilliant attorney with a talent for analyzing emotions, adding depth to the courtroom battles.
  5. Miles Edgeworth: Phoenix's rival and a formidable prosecutor known for his meticulous approach to cases.
